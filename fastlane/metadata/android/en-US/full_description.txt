**This game is in German only.** The interface, all 58 quests, the glossary and the quizzes are German; there is no English version and none is planned for now.

A game about money for teenagers, aged roughly 13 and up. Budget your allowance, save, invest, wait — and watch what grows.

Important: this is a learning simulation, not investment advice. Every price, company and return in the game is fictional. Real money behaves differently.

WHAT YOU GET

• A day cycle instead of real time — nothing happens while the phone is in your pocket. A day ends when you tap "sleep".
• Nine asset classes with distinct character: savings account, ETFs, single stocks, crypto, precious metals, real estate, pension products, collectibles, trees — each on its own island.
• 58 story quests about everyday traps: subscriptions, phishing, fake shops, instalment plans, peer pressure, a first part-time job, tax allowances.
• A daily question, a knowledge quiz, and a glossary that records what you have learned.
• Time travel: fast-forward years and review your wealth over time.
• A bridge to real life: log real savings goals, have a parent confirm them, get rewarded in game.

PRINCIPLES

• No real brands, no real securities — everything is fictional.
• No loot boxes, no in-app purchases, no gambling mechanics.
• No tracking, no analytics, no cloud. The app sends nothing.
• Gender-neutral by design: the app never asks, and female and male characters cost the same and stand side by side.

PRIVACY

Everything stays on the device. No account, no sign-up, no personal data. The app does not even request the INTERNET permission, so Android prevents it from opening any connection.

SUPPORTING THE PROJECT

The game is free, ad-free and has no in-app purchases. Anyone who wants to support its development can leave a tip — with nothing in return: it unlocks nothing and changes nothing in the game. The link lives in the source repository; inside the app it sits under Settings and is visible to adults only.
